# solar-challenge-week0

Week 0 Challenge - Cross-Country Solar Farm Analysis for MoonLight Energy Solutions. EDA and comparison of solar potential in Benin, Sierra Leone, and Togo.

## Project Overview
Analysis of solar farm data from Benin, Sierra Leone, and Togo to identify high-potential regions for solar investment for MoonLight Energy Solutions.

## Key Findings
- **Highest Solar Potential**: Benin with average GHI of 236.23 W/m²
- **Most Consistent**: Sierra Leone shows lowest variability
- **Statistical Significance**: Yes (p-value: 0.0000)
- **Recommendation**: Prioritize investment in Benin with storage solutions
